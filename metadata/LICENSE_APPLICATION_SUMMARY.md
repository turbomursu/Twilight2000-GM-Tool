## 📞 Common Questions

**Q: Do I have to pay?**
A: No! GPL v3 is free forever.

**Q: Do I have to share my campaigns?**
A: No! GPL covers the tool code, not your data.

**Q: Can I use commercially?**
A: If unmodified, yes. If modified, you must share improvements.

**Q: Who owns the copyright?**
A: Contributors own copyright for their contributions.

**Q: Can I make a closed-source fork?**
A: No, GPL requires sharing the license.

**Q: What if I improve it?**
A: You can distribute improvements under GPL v3 to others.

**Q: Do I need to credit you?**
A: Not legally, but appreciated! (Include LICENSE file)

**Q: Can this change?**
A: Not for existing versions. Future versions could theoretically differ, but unlikely.
