Further Character Development

Experience: During gameplay, you'll have the opportunity to gain
experience and spend that experience to improve your character. For
more info, see pg. 40.

Increasing CUF: During any session where you successfully roll your
CUF, roll your CUF base die. On a result of 1, your CUF increases a
step, to a maximum rating of A. Each time your CUF increases, roll your
EMP base die; on a success, your Empathy is reduced one step.
Group Morale Changes: After each session, your unit morale may
change in accordance with the events in the session. Consult pg. 41.
Other: After each session, you may choose a new moral code if you've
betrayed your existing code during that session. You may also opt to
choose a new big dream or choose a new buddy (only one at a time)
after each session.

Skills and Skill Rolls

When your character takes an action, grab two dice to make a Skill Roll. One represents the dice
size of your attribute rating, and the other represents the dice size of your skill rating. Attributes are
rated A-D, and skills are rated A-F, with F representing an untrained roll of only the related attribute
dice.

If a 6 or higher, or , is rolled on either die, that represents a "Success". If you roll a 10 or higher,
that counts as two successes, meaning that if you roll very lucky, you could potentially net four
successes from one skill roll. The results of your roll will often vary based on your number of
successes.

Failure to roll a 6 or higher on either dice represents a failure. If you want to try to roll again, often
you can "Push" a roll (described in detail on p. 45), or the Referee will determine the consequences
of your failure.

When multiple members of the party are working together on one task, assign one character the task
of handling the Group Roll. Others in the party can often "Help" that character, which can add
positive modifiers to their roll.

Sometimes, your rolls will have Positive or Negative Modifiers. These modifiers represent a change
in the dice size that you are rolling. A positive modifier raises the dice to the next size up, anda
negative one lowers the dice size by one step.

Sometimes you will need to roll an Opposed Roll to see which character comes out on top in a given
event. An opposed roll (which can sometimes be different skill rolls for different characters) is
comprised of you and the other character both rolling for skills. Whichever character ends up with
more successes "wins" the scenario.

Skills

Close Combat: This skill encompasses your character's ability to fight both with and without a melee
weapon.

Heavy Weapons: This skill determines how proficient your character is with weapons such as
machine guns, rocket launchers, ATGMs, vehicle cannons, artillery, etc.

Stamina: Your Stamina represents your character's toughness and endurance.

Driving: Your driving skill determines your ability to perform advanced driving techniques (beyond
normal operation of a vehicle) for all manner of wheeled and tracked vehicles. Some specialties apply
for specific types of vehicles.

Mobility: This skill is used anytime your character runs fast, jumps, climbs, etc.

Ranged Combat: Ranged Combat is your character's proficiency with small arms of all kinds -
pistols, rifles, shotguns, bow and crossbows, etc. Note that machine guns fall under the Heavy
Weapons skill.

Recon: Recon allows your character to spot enemies and move stealthily. It plays a heavy part in
performing and avoiding ambushes.

Survival: This skill represents your ability to find food and shelter in the wild.

Tech: Tech allows you to repair damaged equipment and scrounge vital parts that your characters
come across in their journey. It also allows for the crafting of new items.

Command: Command impacts your ability to rally your team mates and effects your unit morale.
Medical Aid: Medical Aid is used to treat injuries on yourself and others, but mostly others.
Persuasion: Persuasion is your ability to get others to do what you want them to do. It can often be
impacted by your bargaining position and your rank.

Encumbrance

Encumbrance in Twilight 2000 is handled in units, with each unit weighing roughly 3kg. Your
character's Strength dice size determines the number of units they can carry without being slowed.
