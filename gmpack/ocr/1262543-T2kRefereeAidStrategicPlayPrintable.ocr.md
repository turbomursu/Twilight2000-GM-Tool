Marching: When marching on foot, players can travel 2 hexes per shift in road
or open terrain. In any other terrain type, players can move only 1 hex per shift.

Forced March: With a successful Stamina roll, characters can
march more than 2 shifts in a day. Each player must roll
successfully, or risk being left behind. Players may even march a 4th
shift in a day, but must roll Stamina with a -2 penalty. A 4th shift of
marching will mean that the character becomes sleep deprived.

Driving: Terrain impacts your speed when driving off-road. Half way through any driving
shift, make a driving roll. A failure causes a Mishap (pg. 142.) Heavy rain gives a -2 modifier
to this roll.

Anytime you change terrain types while driving, make an additional driving roll with the same
consequences for failure. Remember, pushing a driving roll can damage your vehicle's

Conditions that Players may suffer from during their travels:

Starving: PCs must eat one ration of food each day. Starvation has several effects:

e You cannot heal damage in any way. You can recover stress normally.

e You suffer 1 point of damage per week. If you are incapacitated by damage while starving, you
die after another week without food.

e Once you eat, you are no longer starving, and heal as normal.

Dehydrated: PCs must drink one ration of water per day. Dehydration has several effects:

e You cannot heal damage in any way. You can recover stress normally.

e You take 1 point of damage per day. If you are incapacitated by damage while dehydrated, you
die after another day without water.

e Once you drink, you are no longer dehydrated, and can heal as normal.

Sleep Deprived: PCs must sleep at least one shift per day. Sleep deprivation has several effects:

« You cannot heal stress. If you are incapacitated by stress while sleep deprived, you must sleep
for one shift before you can heal stress.

e You take one point of stress per day. If incapacitated by stress while sleep deprived, you
collapse and sleep for one shift. You cannot be woken during this time.

e Once you sleep for one shift, you are no longer sleep deprived and can heal stress as normal.

Hypothermic: When cold and lacking adequate protection (at Referee's discretion), you must roll
Stamina. Protective goods can offer bonuses to the roll. Ex. On a chill fall day, roll once per day. In
winter, you might roll once a shift. A failed roll leads to Hypothermia. Hypothermia has several
effects:

e You immediately suffer 1 damage and 1 stress.

e You cannot heal damage or stress.

e You can even hallucinate - details are at the Referee's discretion.

e You must continue rolling Stamina at the same time interval. Each time you fail the roll, you
suffer 1 damage and 1 stress. If you become incapacitated while hypothermic, at the next
interval you die.

e Once you have been warmed up, even if just by a campfire, you are able to heal again as
normal.
