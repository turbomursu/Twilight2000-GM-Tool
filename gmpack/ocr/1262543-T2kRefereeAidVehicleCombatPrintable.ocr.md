Additional Attack Information | |
Reloading: Reloading vehicle weapons usually takes a slow action. Having a loader allows the
weapon to be fired every round.

Bailing Out: Anytime a vehicle is penetrated, all occupants must make a CUF roll. Failure means
they must immediately spend a slow action exiting the vehicle.

Called Shots: You can make called shots (which never penetrate) on external components of a
vehicle with a -2 modifier. The small arms modifier of +2 when firing at vehicles negates this. You can
only hit the tracks or wheels when making a called shot from the sides.

Disabling Vehicles: Hits to the Engine, Fuel Tank, Suspension, Tracks, or Wheels of a vehicle can
render the vehicle unable to move until it is repaired. A hit to the fuel tank or the ammunition on a
vehicle can render the vehicle permanently destroyed and it cannot be repaired (it can still be
scavenged for parts, however).

Explosive Hits: If a weapon with Blast Power penetrates the vehicle, all occupants suffer the effects
from the explosion as well as any direct hits. The blast damage is not decreased by the armor.
Targeting a hex: When targeting a hex, ignore the target hex terrain type when calculating the to-hit
dice being used. Extra successes beyond the first have no effect.

Ambush: You cannot ambush from a moving vehicle, but you can waylay enemies if you spend a
stretch in preparation. pg. 61.

Component List - Hits and Effects:

Fuel: A penetration of 1 or more will cause the fuel tank to leak. The maximum fuel capacity is
reduced by half until repaired, and the fuel tank will immediately drain to that level if it has fuel in
excess of 50% in it. Any excess damage beyond 1 will carry on to other components.

If penetrated by an explosive attack, roll for the blast power as normal. If there are any successes,
the fuel catches fire. All crew are exposed to fire with intensity C, and must make a CUF roll or bail
out on their very next turn. A fuel fire destroys the vehicle beyond repair, and the fire will burn for a
stretch.

Engine: Each point of penetration damage will reduce the reliability rating of the vehicle by 1. If
reduced to zero, the vehicle becomes inoperable. Any excess damage will not transfer to other
components, being stopped by the engine block.

Ammunition: The main armament's ammo has been hit. If 1 point of penetration is done, the
ammunition is halved. If 2 points are done, all ammunition is lost. Excess penetration damage will
carry on to the next component.

Ammunition for heavy weapons also has a risk of exploding. 1 point of penetration damage carries a
50% chance of exploding (1D3). 2 points of penetration damage will always cause the ammo to
explode. If the ammunition explodes, it causes an explosion with the blast power of the ammunition
exploding, raised one step (up to an A). The vehicle is also destroyed beyond repair.

Cargo: The internal cargo hold is struck, and a random piece of cargo suffers damage (pg. 91 for
effects of damage on gear). If the item is destroyed, any additional damage transfers to other
components.

Driver: The driver is hit, and suffers damage normally. Any excess damage transfers to another
component. Any other occupant of the vehicle can become driver with a fast action.

Passenger: A random passenger is hit, and suffers damage normally. Any excess damage transfers
to another component, including other passengers if present.

Gunner: The gunner is hit, and suffers damage normally. Any excess damage transfers to another
component.

Commander: The commander is hit, and suffers damage normally. Any excess damage transfers to
another component.

Radio: Each point of damage reduces the reliability rating of the radio by 1. Normally radios only
have a reliability rating of 1. If reduced to 0, the radio becomes inoperable until it is repaired. Any
excess damage transfers to another component.

Track/Wheel: Can only be hit by side attacks. For each point of damage in excess of half of the side
armor of the vehicle (rounded up and then modified by the armor modifier), the reliability rating of
the vehicle is reduced by 1. If reduced to 0, the vehicle becomes inoperable. If the attack's damage
doesn't exceed half of the side armor level, then the attack does no damage.

Weapon: A random weapon on the vehicle has its reliability rating reduced by 1. If reduced to 0, the
weapon becomes inoperable until its repaired. Tank guns are armored, so damage to them is
reduced by half of the front armor of the vehicle (rounded up, then modified by armor modifier).

Fire Control System (FCS): If the FCS suffers 1 point of damage or more, it is rendered
inoperable.

Antenna: If the antenna suffers 1 point of damage or more, it is rendered inoperable.

External Stores: A random piece of external cargo is hit. For damage effects on gear, please see
pg. 91.

Exposed Passenger: A random exposed passenger is hit (or partially exposed), and suffers
damage normally.

Ricochet: The attack bounces off in a random direction. Anyone on foot in the hex must make a
CUF roll.
