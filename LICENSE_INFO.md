## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Can I use this commercially?**
A: If you use it unmodified, yes! If you modify it and distribute it, you must share the modifications.

**Q: Do I have to open-source my campaign data?**
A: No! GPL covers the tool code, not data created with it. Your campaigns stay private.

**Q: Can I make a closed-source fork?**
A: No, that would violate GPL v3. You must share modifications if you distribute.

**Q: What if I combine it with another project?**
A: The combined project must be GPL v3 compatible. All code must be open source.

**Q: Who owns the copyright?**
A: Contributors own copyright for their contributions. It's protected under GPL v3.

**Q: Can I charge money?**
A: You can charge for distribution (e.g., on a CD), support, customization, or hosting. You cannot charge for the license itself.

**Q: Do I need to credit you?**
A: Not legally required by GPL v3, but appreciated! Include a notice in your modified versions.

**Q: What happens if I violate GPL?**
A: The licensor can sue you and/or demand you comply. However, we're committed to community benefit, not litigation.

**Q: Can you change the license later?**
A: No, existing releases stay GPL v3. Future versions could theoretically use different licenses, but it's unlikely.
